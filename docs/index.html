C quotes both sides of every held race (and bids in the 5 non-held races furthest below Kalshi) around a reservation price = Kalshi fair (mid, overround removed) skewed against our inventory; no takes. Risk-adding quotes only below 2,000 shares per race, so larger positions unwind through the risk-cutting quotes. Quotes never make the pair cheaper for others. At risk = shares that pay 0 if the underdog wins. Holding: the race still has arb pairs. Closing: no pairs left, the leftover is unwound slowly with skewed quotes. Trades are attributed exactly from our order tags since tagging began; earlier B trades are estimated (single legs on B races). Expected gain uses Kalshi's current probabilities, not a locked profit.
